# Awesome-Digital-Work-Instructions

## Top Digital Work Instructions Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Visual SOPs, Shop-Floor Guidance, Connected Worker Apps, Step-by-Step Assembly & Manufacturing Training*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Digital Work Instructions**. These systems replace paper SOPs with interactive, visual, version-controlled guidance—often with photos, AR, skills tracking, and real-time completion data for operators on the shop floor.



**Examples** include Tulip, Poka, VKS, Dozuki, SwipeGuide, Proceedix, Azumuta, Manual.to, REWO, Trainual Manufacturing, Augmentir, Intoware, and Operations1 (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full commercial connected-worker suites dominate. Open options include **visual instruction builders** (Vision Prep, SOP Rocket), **open MES platforms** with work-order guidance (Carbon, OpenMES), and **wiki/documentation** stacks adapted for SOPs. This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Tulip](https://tulip.co/)**  

  No-code frontline operations platform—apps for work instructions, quality, and machine connectivity without traditional MES complexity.



- **[Poka, Augmentir, Azumuta, Operations1](https://www.poka.io/)**  

  Connected-worker platforms combining digital work instructions, skills management, issue capture, and training on the floor.



- **[Dozuki, VKS, SwipeGuide, Proceedix, Manual.to, REWO](https://www.dozuki.com/)**  

  Specialists in visual SOPs, standard work, and step-by-step manufacturing guidance with strong authoring and version control.



- **[Intoware, Trainual Manufacturing & related](https://www.intoware.com/)**  

  Mobile and AR-oriented work instruction and training tools for complex assembly and field operations.



- **[Other commercial digital work instruction platforms](https://tulip.co/)**  

  Additional frontline and lean manufacturing software with SOP and checklist modules.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Vision Prep](https://github.com/Azzbo77/Vision-Prep)**  

  Open-source platform for visual step-by-step assembly instructions—photo annotations, modular steps, progress tracking, and live build reporting.



- **[SOP Rocket](https://github.com/dylanpjenkins/sop-rocket)**  

  Open-source desktop app for creating Standard Operating Procedures—screenshots, annotations, workflow recording, and PDF/DOCX/Markdown export.



- **[Carbon](https://carbon.ms/)**  

  Open-source manufacturing ERP/MES/QMS—digital travelers and work instructions on a single live factory model (Community edition available).



- **[OpenMES](https://getopenmes.com/)**  

  Free open-source Manufacturing Execution System—work orders, shop-floor visibility, and process guidance with self-hosted deployment.



- **[BookStack, XWiki](https://github.com/BookStackApp/BookStack)**  

  Open wiki platforms frequently used to structure hierarchical SOPs, work instructions, and manufacturing knowledge bases.



- **[Odoo Manufacturing / quality modules](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Open ERP with work orders, routing, and quality checks that can host digital instruction content for operators.



- **[Open Factory Twin (work instruction components)](https://openfactorytwin.github.io/ofact/)**  

  Open digital twin framework for production that includes tools to pass work instructions back to the physical world.



- **[MediaWiki / DokuWiki SOP deployments](https://github.com/search?q=SOP+OR+work+instruction+wiki+manufacturing)**  

  Community patterns for versioned, searchable standard work on classic open wiki engines.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Visual assembly guidance**: Vision Prep for photo-rich, modular build steps.

- **SOP authoring**: SOP Rocket for fast screenshot-based procedure creation.

- **MES-integrated instructions**: Carbon or OpenMES for work orders tied to execution data.

- **Knowledge base**: BookStack/XWiki for controlled document hierarchies.

- **Composable stacks**: Wiki/SOP authoring → tablet browser on the line → optional open MES for completion tracking.

- Commercial platforms still lead in AR, offline mobile apps, skills matrices, and multi-site governance.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

**Vision Prep** and **SOP Rocket** for instruction content; **Carbon** / **OpenMES** / **Odoo** for execution context; **BookStack** for controlled documentation.  

Commercial platforms (Tulip, Poka, Dozuki, VKS, Augmentir, etc.) deliver polished operator UX and analytics.  

Small manufacturers often combine open wikis with tablets; larger plants adopt commercial connected-worker suites. Fully open stacks are viable for pilot lines and cost-sensitive shops with internal IT support.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Work instructions affect product quality, safety, and compliance. Incorrect or outdated steps can cause defects or injury. Maintain revision control, training records, and validation against actual processes. Regulated industries may require validated systems and electronic signatures.

- Open-source tools offer control and low cost but place content quality, security, and uptime on the operator. Commercial platforms shift product and support burden to the vendor. Neither replaces skilled supervision and continuous improvement culture.
